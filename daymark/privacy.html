On this page

Daymark is a calendar, tasks and notes app for iPhone and iPad, made by Tony Aboud ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Daymark does not collect your data. Everything you put in it stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

1. What Daymark stores, and where

  • Your content — tasks, notes, events you create in Daymark, courses, semesters, attachments and settings — is stored on your device, inside Daymark's private storage.
  • Backups — Daymark makes automatic backups on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, copies them to your own iCloud Drive (the "Daymark" folder). Apple stores these under your Apple Account; we cannot see them.
  • Apple Calendar and Reminders — if you allow it, Daymark reads and writes events in Apple Calendar and can import Apple Reminders. That information stays in Apple's apps and your iCloud; Daymark only keeps its own links (such as which note belongs to which event) on your device.

2. What we collect

Nothing. Daymark has no accounts, no servers that receive your content, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or crash-reporting libraries. We never see your calendar, tasks or notes, and we don't sell or share any data — there is none to share.

3. Permissions Daymark asks for

  • Calendars — to show and save your events. Optional: without it, events are kept inside Daymark.
  • Reminders — only if you choose to import your Apple Reminders.
  • Notifications and alarms — so your reminders can reach you, including alarms that ring through silent mode for important items.
  • Photos and files — only for items you choose to attach to a note.

Each permission is asked for when it is first needed, and you can change it any time in the iOS Settings app.

4. Children

Daymark is a general productivity app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app (they wait in the Trash for 30 days, then are removed), or delete the app to remove everything stored on the device. Backups in iCloud Drive can be deleted from the Files app.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: support@classmateapp.org.
