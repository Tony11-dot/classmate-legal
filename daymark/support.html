Need help with Daymark? Write to support@classmateapp.org — we usually answer within a day or two. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were trying to do.

Common questions

A reminder didn't ring

Open Settings › Reminder health check in Daymark. It checks notification permissions, Focus and Scheduled Summary, alarms, Low Power Mode and background refresh, and tells you exactly what to change. You can also send yourself a test notification from there.

Where are my events?

Daymark shows Apple Calendar events once you allow Calendar access (Settings › Calendar › Apple Calendar). Without access, events you create are kept inside Daymark.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Daymark backs up every day and keeps a copy in iCloud Drive › Daymark. On the new phone, open Settings › Sync & data › Backups to restore.

Can I use Daymark in Hebrew?

Yes — choose עברית in Settings › Language. The whole app switches to right-to-left, with Hebrew dates and Israeli holidays.
