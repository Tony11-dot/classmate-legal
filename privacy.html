ClassMate and ClassNotes Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what data the ClassMate mobile application ("the App") collects, why we collect it, and how we use it. The App is operated by Tony Aboud ("we," "us"). By using the App you consent to the practices described here.

1. Data we collect

We collect the following categories of data so the App can function:

  • Contact info — email address, name, phone number. A phone number is verified by SMS code when you link it after signing in; it is used for account security (2FA linking) and SMS password reset. The same phone number may back more than one account (for example, family members who share a phone).
  • User content — direct messages, voice messages, photos, files, announcement posts, and school mail ("CMail") messages with their attachments that you create or send inside the App.
  • Student records — for student users: grades, attendance, schedule, assignments, exams, certificates issued by your school's teachers.
  • Parent linkage — for parent users: the list of children you are approved to view. School staff (teachers and administrators) at your child's school can see your name, phone number, and email as the child's emergency/contact details.
  • Staff notes — teachers and administrators can write internal pedagogical notes about a student. Each note is private to the staff member who wrote it — other staff, students, and parents cannot read it — and it stays inside the student's school.
  • AI conversation data — the messages, photos and documents that students and teachers send to the NOVA AI tutor and the responses received. We use this data to deliver tutoring responses through Anthropic's Claude API and to track token usage. Only what you write or attach is sent to the AI — never your name, school, grades or other account details.
  • NOVA safety screening — every message to NOVA is first checked automatically (by keyword rules and by an AI classifier running on Anthropic's Claude API) for signs of distress or self-harm, sexual content, threats and hate. Requests in the harmful categories are refused. If a student's message suggests they may be in distress or in danger, NOVA answers with help resources (such as ERAN 1201 and SAHAR), and the student's school administrators receive an alert naming the student so a trusted adult can check in; the alert does not include the message itself.
  • Purchase data — subscription tier, top-up purchases, and entitlement state, processed through Apple's StoreKit and synchronised via RevenueCat.
  • Identifiers — a user ID generated by our server. We do not collect device advertising identifiers (IDFA / IDFV).
  • Diagnostics — crash logs and basic error telemetry to keep the App working.

2. Why we collect it

  • App functionality — to display your schedule, grades, messages, and announcements; to authenticate you; to power the NOVA AI tutor; to process purchases.
  • Student safety — to screen NOVA conversations and alert a student's school when a message suggests the student may be at risk, as described above.
  • Account management — login, password reset, role-based permissions (student, teacher, parent, secretary, admin).
  • Communication — sending in-app notifications when something happens that involves you (a new grade, an announcement, a new direct message).
  • Service improvement — anonymised error and performance data so we can fix bugs.

We do not use your data for advertising or for any kind of cross-app tracking. We do not sell your data.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with the third-party processors required to run the App:

  • Anthropic — in ClassMate, your messages to NOVA are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate tutoring responses. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Groq — the in-app Support assistant in ClassMate, and in ClassNotes the questions you ask NOVA on the free plan, are answered by a model hosted by Groq. See Groq's privacy policy.
  • Apple / Google speech recognition — when you dictate a question to NOVA, your device's built-in speech recognition (Apple or Google) turns your voice into text; only the text reaches ClassMate, and no recording is stored.
  • Anthropic (ClassNotes) — on a NOVA Plus or Pro plan, the questions you ask NOVA in ClassNotes are answered by Anthropic's Claude API, up to the plan's daily allowance, and by Groq after that.
  • Apple / Google Play / RevenueCat — in-app purchase transactions are processed by Apple or Google and synced through RevenueCat. See Apple, Google, and RevenueCat.
  • Resend — transactional emails (password reset, account verification). See Resend's privacy policy.
  • Twilio — SMS password reset codes when you choose SMS as your reset channel. See Twilio's privacy policy.
  • Railway — our application server and database are hosted with Railway. See Railway's privacy policy.
  • Firebase (Google) — push notification delivery. A per-device push token is stored so we can deliver notifications to your device. See Firebase privacy.
  • Sentry — crash reports and error telemetry (device model, OS version, stack traces — never passwords or message content). See Sentry's privacy policy.

Within your school, your data is visible according to your role. Teachers and administrators see their school's students — grades, attendance, insights, and the student's parent contact details — to do their job. Staff notes about a student are visible only to the staff member who wrote them. Parents see their own children's records (linked through an approved parent-child relationship). Students only see their own data. NOVA conversations are private to the student or teacher who has them: they are not shown to parents, teachers or administrators (only the safety alert described above reaches the school). School administrators can switch NOVA off for their school's students or teachers, and students below grade 7 do not have NOVA, following the Israeli Ministry of Education's guidelines for generative AI. School mail (CMail) is delivered only to the audience its sender chose inside the school. No data ever crosses school boundaries.

4. Data retention

We retain your account data while your account is active. In-app notifications are deleted after 180 days and usage analytics after 365 days. When you delete your account (in the App, or by contacting us at the email below), your personal data — including your NOVA conversations — is deleted, except where retention is required for legal or accounting reasons (for example, purchase records for tax purposes). A student account that a school manages is deleted by the school's administrator, because the student's grades and attendance are school records. Grades, classes and assignments a teacher created belong to the school and pass to a school administrator when the teacher's account is deleted. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel it in your store settings.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, request correction or deletion, or withdraw your consent at any time by emailing the contact address below. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Children's privacy

ClassMate is intended for students at participating schools, including users under 16. Student accounts are created by the school administrator on the student's behalf. No advertising or third-party tracking is used in the App. Parents linked to a student account can see that student's school records (such as grades, attendance and schedule) and message the student's teachers; a student's NOVA conversations stay private to the student.

7. Security

We use HTTPS in transit, password hashing (bcrypt) and field-level encryption of personal details at rest, JWT-based authentication, and role-based authorisation on every endpoint, with each school's data kept separate from every other school's. Passwords and tokens are never logged, and error reports never include request contents. The server hardens against brute-force attacks with per-IP rate limits on credential endpoints.

8. International transfers

Our server is hosted in the United States. By using the App you consent to your data being processed in the United States and in the countries where our processors operate.

9. Legal basis and governing law

We process personal data in accordance with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, including Amendment 13 (in force August 2025), under which student educational records are treated as sensitive information. School records (grades, attendance, schedules) are collected and owned by the school; ClassMate processes them on the school's behalf and instructions. We maintain data-security practices appropriate to a database containing sensitive information, honour data-subject rights under the Law (access, correction, deletion), and report security incidents as the Law requires. This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Israel.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, where appropriate, notify users in-app.

11. ClassNotes

ClassNotes is a separate note-taking app from the same operator. It works without an account. Your notebooks are stored on your device, as files you own.

  • Account (optional) — if you create one, we store your email address, name and a hashed password. You can delete the account inside the app (Settings); that removes the copies of your notebooks held on our server. Notebooks on your device are not deleted.
  • Notebook copies (with an account) — when you are signed in, rendered pages, covers, and attachments such as voice notes, files and links are sent to our server so they appear in the ClassMate ClassNotes tab. Your settings are also stored so they follow you between devices.
  • Shared notebooks (only the ones you share) — when you share a notebook and invite someone by email, that notebook's content — ink, text boxes and other page content, page settings and media such as photos, files and voice notes — is sent to our server and relayed live to the people you invited, who keep a copy on their own devices. The server keeps the notebook's change history so members can catch up after being offline. The invitation email carries only your name and the notebook's title. While a shared notebook is open, who is present in it is relayed to its members and never stored. You can remove a member or stop sharing at any time, which stops them receiving changes; deleting your account removes the notebooks you shared and your memberships in others'.
  • NOVA (AI), only with your permission — nothing is sent to an AI model until you agree in the app, and you can withdraw that in Settings. When you ask NOVA a question, the question is sent, plus any snip you drew around, and, only if you choose "Read this notebook", text from that notebook's pages. These are sent through our server to Groq on the free plan, or to Anthropic (Claude) on NOVA Plus and Pro, to produce the answer. For paid plans we count how many Claude answers your account uses each day and what they cost, so the daily allowance can be applied; we do not store the questions or answers. On the free plan, what the model read off a snip is held in the server's memory for up to 30 minutes so follow-up questions about it aren't read again, and is then discarded. If you have no account and add your own Groq key, requests go directly to Groq using your key, which is kept in your device's Keychain.
  • NOVA plans (purchases) — NOVA Plus and Pro are bought through Apple and recorded by RevenueCat under your ClassNotes account's id, so your plan follows your account. We read which plan is active and when it renews or ends; we never see your payment details.
  • On your device only — handwriting recognition and search run on your device with Apple's Vision. Diagnostics (crash and performance reports from Apple's MetricKit) stay on your device and are shared only if you choose to send them from Support.
  • iCloud (optional) — if you turn on iCloud sync, copies of your notebooks are kept in your own iCloud Drive and are never sent to us.
  • No tracking — ClassNotes has no advertising, no analytics SDK and no cross-app tracking.

To ask for your ClassNotes data to be deleted, use Delete Account in the app or email the address below.
